## Summary
This is the **baseline run** — the current 51-item inventory is recorded as the reference, so nothing is "new" yet; future runs diff against it. The main risks are **fragmentation in the Chat bubble family** (5 separate bubble components that re-model the same in/out distinction), a couple of **content-named variants and duplicated frames**, and a small set of **local tokens defined outside the AOH library** that need a governance decision.

## 📐 Design-system adherence (AOH UI/ShadCN)
**Good:** The section consistently uses bound design tokens rather than raw values —
- **Type:** Geist ramp (`text-xs`…`text-xl`, plus `ShadCN typography/h1,h2`).
- **Color:** `color-bg*`, `color-text*`, `color-border*`, and semantic `color-text-error/warning/info/success`.
- **Spacing/Radius/Size:** `Spacing/spacing-2xs…lg`, `Radius/radius-button/input/sm/round`, `Size/size-button-lg`, `Size/size-input-md`, `Size/size-avatar-sm`.

**Drift to watch:**
- **Local feature tokens** (`chat/*`, `comms/*`, `kbd/*`) bypass the library — see Flag #3.
- **Possible local re-implementation of `chip`** — `77:251` Priority / Pill and `127:109` Filter Chip may be re-built locally when AOH provides a `chip` component (ties to the "Chip or tag?" note). Verify whether they should be built from the AOH `chip`. (Needs verification.)
- Per-component hardcoded-value checks (detached instances, off-token hex) are **not exhaustively verified in this baseline run** — scheduled runs spot-check a rotating sample via `get_design_context`.

## ✏️ What can change (advisory — not applied)
1. **Unify the Chat bubble family** into one variant set (Type × Direction × State) — biggest structural win (Duplicates #1–3).
2. **Componentize the viewfinder**; dedupe StatusBar / Tab bar usage (Duplicates #4, #6).
3. **Resolve & remove** the "Chip or tag?" note and align Priority/Filter chips to the AOH `chip` (Flag #1, adherence).
4. **Govern the local tokens** — promote to AOH or document as sanctioned extensions (Flag #3).
5. **Tidy the canvas** — remove loose annotation text; verify/clear the empty BG overlay; clarify `Description Flow`.
