## Summary
Since the last recorded baseline (2026-07-01, 73 components, flat list), the watched node has been
**completely restructured** into a "Mobile Component Library" with a 9-section hierarchy (Foundations ·
Global · Shared · Comms · Chat · Incident · Map · Resources · Login) and now carries a self-reported count
of **113 components**. This is a large, positive step toward organization — but **3 of the 9 labeled
sections are currently empty** (zero child components), and long-standing chat-bubble fragmentation has
gotten worse, not better, in the new structure. A standard new/removed diff against the flat 2026-07-01
baseline is not meaningful across a full re-architecture, so this run instead audits the new structure
directly and re-baselines for tomorrow.

## ✏️ What can change (advisory — not applied)
1. **Resolve the Comms/Resources gap first** — confirm whether those components were moved, are mid-migration, or were dropped, before any further reorg work continues on top of them.
2. Consolidate the 8-way chat-bubble fragmentation into one variant-bound `Chat / Bubble` set.
3. Rename `Option Context menu`'s `Property 1` to something meaningful (e.g. restore `Source=Pinned|Recent`).
4. Sweep the naming inconsistencies (delimiter style, casing, the "Segemented" typo, restoring `/ Empty State`'s prefix) in one pass — low effort, meaningfully improves scanability.
